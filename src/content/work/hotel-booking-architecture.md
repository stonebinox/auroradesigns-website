## Context

The hospitality industry runs on established RMS, PMS, and GDS frameworks. A new hotel-booking system has to integrate with those legacy standards, not work around them.

## Approach

Embedded as lead architect, designing the system architecture to sit inside the existing RMS, PMS, and GDS frameworks the industry runs on — building within established, legacy hospitality-tech standards rather than replacing them.

## Status

In active development. This is an architecture-stage engagement; outcome and adoption will follow once the platform is in production.
