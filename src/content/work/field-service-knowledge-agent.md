## Context

Field technicians needed procedure and compliance information on-site, but the existing document library was scattered across three repositories with no cross-search. Technicians either called back to office or worked from printed binders, creating delays and a risk of acting on outdated information.

## Approach

Aurora embedded with the operations and IT teams to build a retrieval agent using MCP and Elasticsearch. Twelve thousand documents were indexed, chunked, and structured for semantic retrieval. A React interface designed for mobile use let technicians ask questions in plain language and receive sourced answers with document references.

## Outcome

The agent reached a working pilot in eight weeks. Field teams used it on the first day of the pilot without training sessions. Response accuracy on compliance queries was validated against the source documents, and the team committed to full rollout across all field regions.
